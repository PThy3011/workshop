## General Feedback

### 1. Working Environment

The working environment at **AWS Vietnam** is friendly, professional, and highly supportive. Team members were always willing to help whenever I encountered difficulties. The workplace was comfortable and encouraged learning and collaboration. I believe organizing more team-building activities would help interns integrate more quickly and strengthen connections within the team.

### 2. Support from Mentor and Team

My mentor provided excellent guidance throughout the internship. They explained technical concepts clearly, encouraged me to think independently before offering solutions, and were always patient when answering my questions. Their support greatly improved both my technical knowledge and problem-solving skills.

### 3. Relevance to My Academic Background

The assigned tasks were closely aligned with my university studies while also exposing me to real-world AWS services that I had previously only learned about in theory. This internship provided valuable practical experience in cloud computing and solution design.

### 4. Learning and Professional Development

During the internship, I gained hands-on experience with various AWS services, cloud architecture design, infrastructure deployment, teamwork, and technical documentation such as worklogs and technical blogs. These experiences significantly enhanced both my technical and professional skills.

### 5. Team Culture and Collaboration

The team maintained a professional yet welcoming working environment. Everyone was supportive, collaborative, and willing to share knowledge, which created a positive learning atmosphere throughout the internship.

---

## Additional Feedback

- **Most satisfying aspect:** Having supportive mentors and the opportunity to gain hands-on experience with AWS services through practical labs and projects.

- **Areas for improvement:** Providing more opportunities for interns to collaborate across teams or participate in additional technical workshops would further enrich the internship experience.

- **Would you recommend this internship to your friends?**  
  **Yes.** I would highly recommend this internship because it offers an excellent learning environment, practical cloud experience, and dedicated mentorship.

---

## Suggestions and Future Expectations

- Organize more hands-on technical workshops and knowledge-sharing sessions.
- Provide opportunities for interns to stay connected with the team after completing the internship for continued learning and professional development.
- **Additional comments:** I sincerely appreciate the opportunity to participate in this meaningful internship program. It has been an invaluable experience that strengthened both my technical expertise and professional skills.

I would like to express my heartfelt gratitude to the entire **First Cloud Journey (FCJ)** team and **AWS Vietnam** for their guidance, support, and encouragement throughout my internship.
