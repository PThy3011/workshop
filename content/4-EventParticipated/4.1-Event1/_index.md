# FCAJ Meetup 2026 – Career Journey in Cloud, DevOps, and Data

On **June 13, 2026**, the **FCAJ Meetup** was successfully held, attracting many students and young professionals passionate about technology. The event provided valuable real-world insights from experienced engineers in the Cloud, AI, DevOps, and Data fields.

## Event Objectives

- Share practical career experiences in the IT industry
- Guide students on career development paths in Cloud, DevOps, and Data Analytics
- Introduce corporate culture in multinational companies (MNCs)
- Provide essential skills needed for the job market
- Inspire lifelong learning and personal branding through the AWS community

## Speakers

- **Kiên & Thọ** – Representatives from First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)
- **Danh Hoàng Hiếu Nghị** – AI Engineer, AWS Community Builder, AWS Student Builder Group Leader
- **Trong H. Truong** – DevOps Engineer at Endava Vietnam
- **Đạt Phạm** – Data Analytics Engineer
- **Cường Nguyễn** – Process Engineer

## Key Highlights

### 1. Journey from First Cloud AI Journey to AWS Partner
**Danh Hoàng Hiếu Nghị** shared his personal journey from a student in the First Cloud AI Journey program to becoming an AWS Community Builder and AWS Partner. His key message: “Getting the job is just a beginning.” He encouraged students to actively build their personal brand through LinkedIn, community activities, and real projects.

### 2. What Does a DevOps Engineer Actually Do?
**Trong H. Truong** gave a realistic view of the DevOps role. He clarified the gap between theory and practice, shared hard-learned lessons (“Things I learned the hard way”), and provided a practical learning roadmap: starting with Linux, Networking, Git, CI/CD, Containers, and Cloud.

### 3. Real Work of a Data Analytics Engineer in MNCs
**Đạt Phạm & Cường Nguyễn** shared their experiences working at Kamereo and Colgate-Palmolive. They emphasized:
- The role of data analysis in supporting business decisions
- Building dashboards and KPIs for operations
- “Storytelling with Data” to turn numbers into business value
- Career growth model: Follower → Learner → Problem Solver → System Thinker → Super Star

### 4. Corporate Culture in Multinational Companies
The speakers discussed:
- Professional recruitment processes in MNCs
- No-Blame Post-Mortem culture
- Caring & Inclusive culture
- The importance of English communication and soft skills
- Long-term career mindset instead of chasing job titles

## Key Takeaways

### Career Development
- Build strong fundamentals before learning advanced technologies
- Actively participate in communities to expand your network
- Take ownership of your learning and build a strong portfolio

### Technical Skills
- Master Linux, Networking, and Git before diving into Kubernetes
- Learn Cloud through hands-on practice
- Build personal projects to gain real experience

### Soft Skills
- Critical thinking
- Effective communication
- Data storytelling
- Problem-solving and system thinking

### Corporate Culture
- Understand MNC recruitment processes
- Focus on learning from mistakes rather than blaming
- Prioritize people and collective values

## Personal Experience

Participating in the **FCAJ Meetup 2026** was a highly valuable learning experience. The speakers shared not only technical knowledge but also honest stories from their real working lives. This helped me gain a clearer understanding of what companies are looking for and how to prepare myself for the job market.

I was especially impressed by the speakers’ sincerity and passion. The event was not only about technical skills but also about mindset, personal branding, and continuous self-development.

## Conclusion

The FCAJ Meetup on June 13, 2026, was a meaningful event that provided both practical knowledge and strong inspiration for young people. It helped attendees gain a clearer vision of their career path in Cloud, DevOps, and Data fields.

Thank you to the organizing team and all the speakers for taking the time to share their valuable experiences.
